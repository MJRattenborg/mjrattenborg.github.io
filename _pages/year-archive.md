I co-organized the PhD Workshop in Labor and Behavioral Economics, held at CEBI (University of Copenhagen) on June 8–9, 2026. The workshop brought together PhD students and recent graduates working at the intersection of behavioral and labor economics.

Keynote Speakers:  
Ingar Haaland (NHH), Benjamin Schoefer (UC Berkeley)

Organizers:  
Simon Cordes (University of Bonn), Ida Maria Hartmann (University of Copenhagen), Valentin Kecht (University of Bonn), Malte Jacob Rattenborg (University of Copenhagen)
